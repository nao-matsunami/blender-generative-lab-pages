# Visual Direction Reset - 2026-09-15

## Diagnosis

The recent organ-motif series has become too similar.

The names and anatomy references changed, but too many works used the same visual grammar:

- red or pink soft mass
- black cleft or central hole
- thick lip or folded rim
- one structural tube, or no tube
- similar centered turntable composition

This made the sequence feel like variations of the same object rather than a growing body of work.

The Blender GUI watcher also appears to have stopped after 2026-09-07. Jobs from 2026-09-09 onward are still pending in `blender-jobs/`, so the recent public pieces are mostly web sketches, not finished Blender output. Until the watcher is active again, daily work should avoid claiming those sketches as fully resolved works.

## New Rule

Do not make another "red blob + black hole + one tube" piece unless the goal is to deliberately repair or deconstruct that pattern.

Every new work must differ from the last three works in at least two of these areas:

- overall silhouette
- topology
- construction method
- surface rhythm
- material treatment
- camera/composition
- interaction behavior

## Required Daily Choice

Each daily pass must choose one primary archetype before building:

- perforated or lattice scaffold
- suspended membrane sheet
- branching structural bundle
- nested shell or cutaway cavity
- radial valve machine
- folded terrain relief
- printable skeletal frame
- transparent layered membrane

The selected archetype must be visible in the work. It cannot only appear in the report text.

## Required Geometry Change

Each daily pass must include at least one construction technique that was not central in the previous day's piece:

- boolean cut or perforation
- curve network with thick silhouette-defining tubes
- fused metaball or merged soft-body mass
- procedural displacement field
- stacked membranes
- lattice ribs
- vertex-color or material-zone variation
- composition/camera change that materially changes the viewing experience

## Vessel And Tube Rule

Thin vessel decoration is suspended. It has looked cheap when it reads as surface debris.

Tubes are allowed only when they are major sculptural structures: roots, bundles, ducts, clamps, arches, handles, or branching supports that define the object's silhouette.

## Palette Rule

Keep the main palette around dark crimson, red-purple, flesh pink, muted rose, black, and bruised shadow tones. Avoid blue and cyan.

To avoid monotony, secondary accents may use bone white, cloudy translucent pink, ochre, violet-black, or dark amber, but they should support the object rather than decorate it.

## Review Gate

Before publishing, write one sentence answering each question:

- What visual pattern from recent works was rejected?
- What is structurally different this time?
- Would the thumbnail be recognizable next to the last three works?
- Is this a finished Blender asset, or only a web sketch pending Blender output?

If the answers are weak, revise the form instead of adding another similar piece.

## Next Strong Directions

1. Ossified Organ Lattice: porous bone-organ scaffold with crimson shadows and actual cut-through openings.
2. Pleated Membrane Curtain: suspended translucent sheets, sagging folds, and dark layered backs.
3. Branch Root Mass: thick ducts or roots as the main silhouette, not surface vessel lines.
4. Cutaway Cavity Object: an opened organ-shell where inner dark layers are more important than the outer blob.
